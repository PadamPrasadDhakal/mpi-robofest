01 / ABOUT THE EVENT

Ideas are good.
Building them is better.

MPI RoboFest 2026 is a four-day celebration of practical learning, creativity and technology. Students will explore robotics, build a Bluetooth-controlled robotic car, discover innovative projects and experience the excitement of a Robo Race.

Organized by Mechinagar Polytechnic Institute, with PI Innovations Pvt. Ltd. as the Technical Partner.
